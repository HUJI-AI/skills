# Doctoral studies at HUJI

The Authority for Research Students (הרשות לתלמידי מחקר) runs doctoral
studies at the Hebrew University: admission, the move from stage A to stage
B, advisory committees, submission and examination of the dissertation. Its
websites explain each step and link the regulations and forms:

- Hebrew: <https://research-students.huji.ac.il/>
- English: <https://en-research-students.huji.ac.il/>

Help candidates, PhD students and supervisors find the current rules for
their situation and prepare what each step needs. There is
no API: the pages, the regulations (PDF) and the forms (Word documents) are
the sources.

## Before you answer

- **Read the current page every time.** Deadlines, page limits, required
  documents and forms change. Fetch the page for the question, answer from
  it, and give its link. Do not answer rules from this skill or from memory.
- **The regulations are the binding text.** The pages summarise the Research
  Students Regulations (תקנון תלמידי מחקר) and the regulations on supervising
  graduate students, both linked from the rules page. The Research Students
  Regulations are numbered by section; when the exact rule matters, quote
  the section. If a page and a regulation seem to differ, show both and
  refer the student to the Authority.
- **Find out where the student stands** before answering: candidate, stage
  A, moving to stage B, stage B, submitting, or under examination; their
  faculty; and whether their field is in the experimental sciences (מדעים
  ניסויים) or the humanities and social sciences (מדעים עיוניים). Page
  limits, forms, procedures and the Authority's offices differ between
  these, and some faculties set their own timelines (the course-of-study
  page names them).
- **People decide.** The supervisor, the advisory committee and the
  Authority approve each step. Explain what is needed and help prepare it;
  do not promise an outcome.
- **The work is the student's.** When helping with a research proposal, a
  progress report or a chapter, keep the student as the author. Ask them to
  check with their supervisor what AI use is acceptable and how to
  acknowledge it. This site's guidance on AI in research:
  <https://ai.huji.ac.il/en/policies/ai-research/>.
- **Personal records are elsewhere.** Registration, status and payments are
  in the university's personal information system, which you cannot see.
  Never ask for ID numbers or passwords, and never fill in or sign a form on
  someone's behalf.

## Common requests

**"What do I need to move to stage B?"** Read the course-of-study page,
section "לקראת המעבר לשלב ב׳" (towards stage B), and the forms for that
stage. List the documents, the deadline, the length limits
for the student's field, the committee's role and whether ethics approval
may be needed, as the page states them. Mention that a faculty may set
earlier dates.

**"Help me prepare my research proposal."** Read the stage B requirements
and the examples of research proposals. Help the student plan the structure,
check it against the stated length and format, and tighten their own
writing. The supervisor approves and signs it.

**"How do I submit my dissertation?"** Ask which format they chose: a
monograph or a collection of articles. Read that format's page, then the
submission and examination page and the forms for submission, and lay out
the steps in order with who signs what. The eligibility page explains the
confirmation of the degree and the graduation ceremony that follow.

**"How do I find a supervisor?"** The finding-a-supervisor page and the
regulations' chapter on supervisors; for candidates, also the admission
requirements and the application documents.

**"I need to stop my studies for a while."** The withdrawal and renewal
page, and the regulations' section on pausing and renewing studies.

**"What funding is there?"** The scholarships page, and the travel-grant
forms for short training abroad.

Give the steps in order, each with its page or form link. Quote numbers
(months, pages, credits) exactly. For anything unusual, suggest the student
contact the Authority; the contact page lists its offices by field.
